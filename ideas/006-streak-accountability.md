# Streak & Accountability

A lightweight accountability layer: streaks that actually sting to break, and
small squads that notice when you vanish.

## Problem

Motivation is a spike; consistency is a curve. Apps show you a streak, but
nobody feels it when you break it — so the streak stops mattering after week two.

## Why now

Streaks are cheap to build and people already post them. The gap is the *social
consequence* — a squad of 3 that gets notified when you miss, and you when they do.

## Ideas

- **Squad of 3–5** — small enough that absence is obvious.
- **Streak stakes** — a tiny weekly buy-in refunded only if everyone showed up.
- **Miss → check-in** — a miss triggers a nudge and a "what happened?" prompt.
- **Recovery, not shame** — 1 rest day allowed per week without killing the streak.

## Notes

- The product is the *notification*, not the dashboard.
- **Metric:** 30-day retention of squad members vs. solo users.
- **Risk:** shame mechanics backfire; keep the tone supportive.
