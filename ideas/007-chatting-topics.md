# Chatting & Topics

A social layer that pairs you with a gym partner near you — or a "blind gym-bro"
match — and gives you topic rooms to hang out in.

## Problem

Lifting alone is isolating, and finding a compatible gym partner is mostly luck.

## Why now

Location + interest matching is table-stakes tech, and group chat rooms are a
proven community format.

## Shape

- Match with gym-bros at nearby gyms.
- **Blind gym-bro**: a blind-date-style match that picks a gym from the map using
  compatible tags, then you both adapt to that gym.
- Topic rooms (e.g. gamer gyms) for group chat.

## Notes

- **Metric:** matches → chats → shared sessions.
- **Risk:** safety and privacy in location matching — build consent and report controls first.
