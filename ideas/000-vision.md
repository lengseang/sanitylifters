# Sanity Lifters — Vision & Roadmap

A gym app that makes lifting feel less lonely and more deliberate. **North star:**
help people lift consistently, with better form, and stay motivated through
community and content.

Ideas on this board are grouped into six pillars, in build order.

## Coach — the moat
AI that watches your form and writes your plan. Build this first; it's the
differentiator.

- **AI Form Coach** — real-time feedback from the camera.
- **AI Suggestion & Planning** — personalized routines from a few inputs.

## Train — the daily utility
Progress and recovery, the reason the app stays open.

- **Schedule & Progress Tracking** — a GitHub-style board of completed sessions.
- **Recovery & Readiness Score** — a daily push / pull back / rest signal.

## Engage — the habit loop
Challenges and streaks that keep people showing up.

- **Daily Workout Challenge** — level-scaled daily tasks with score earning.
- **Streak & Accountability** — streaks that sting to break and squads that notice.

## Connect — the community
Matching and rooms that end the loneliness of lifting alone.

- **Chatting & Topics** — nearby gym-bros, "blind gym-bro" matches, topic rooms.

## Content — the discovery feed
Guides and trends that teach and entertain.

- **Lifting Guides & Tutorials** — image and video instruction for every lift.
- **Trends & Viral Workouts** — a curated feed of what's trending and worth doing.

## Monetize — the business
Turn coaches into revenue.

- **Coach Program Marketplace** — programs that live in-app, not as PDFs.

## Priority legend

- **p0** — core loop, build first.
- **p1** — retention and differentiation, build next.
- **p2** — scale and revenue, build later.
