# Chatting & Topics

A social layer that pairs you with a gym partner near you — or a "blind gym-bro"
match — and gives you topic rooms to hang out in.

## Problem

Lifting alone is isolating, and finding a compatible gym partner is mostly luck.

## Target user

- **The new-in-town lifter** who wants a training partner.
- **The shy beginner** who would train with a stranger if the match was made for them.
- **The niche enthusiast** (gamer gyms, powerlifting, calisthenics) who wants a room.

## Why now

Location + interest matching is table-stakes tech, and group chat rooms are a
proven community format.

## Core experience

1. Match with gym-bros at nearby gyms.
2. **Blind gym-bro** — a blind-date-style match that picks a gym from the map using
   compatible tags, then you both adapt to that gym.
3. Topic rooms (e.g. gamer gyms) for group chat.

## User stories

- As a new-in-town lifter, I want to find a gym-bro near me, so I'm not always alone.
- As a shy beginner, I want a "blind gym-bro" match made for me, so I don't have to
  awkwardly ask.
- As a gamer who lifts, I want a room with people like me, so the gym feels social.

## MVP scope

**In:** location + tag matching, chat between matches, a handful of seeded topic rooms.

**Out:** full maps integration, video calls, event scheduling.

## Success metrics

- Matches → conversations → shared sessions funnel.
- Weekly active rooms and messages per room.
- Match-to-meetup conversion.

## Risks

- **Safety and privacy** in location matching — consent, blocking, and reporting first.
- **Ghost-town rooms** — seed and moderate a few rooms before opening the floodgates.

## Open questions

- Do we reveal exact gym location or keep it approximate until mutual consent?
- Is matching 1:1 or should we start with rooms only?
